Privacy Policy for APlusPlan

Last updated: September 2026

APlusPlan ("the app") is developed by Gospro LLC.

Summary

APlusPlan does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no account system.

Data the app stores

The courses, categories, assignments, grades, and settings you enter are stored only in a local database on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not sent to Gospro LLC, to any third party, or to any server
  • is only accessible to you, on your device
  • is deleted if you uninstall the app

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, and has no technical ability to collect:

  • your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • your location
  • advertising identifiers
  • usage analytics or crash reports
  • any data from other apps or services

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect data), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: support@gosprollc.com
