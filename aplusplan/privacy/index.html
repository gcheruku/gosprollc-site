Privacy Policy for APlusPlan

Last updated: October 2026

APlusPlan ("the app") is developed by Gospro LLC.

Summary

APlusPlan does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no analytics, no advertising, and no account system, and it never sends your data anywhere. The only thing that goes online is the store’s own purchase service, if you choose to buy Pro (see “Purchases”).

Data the app stores

The courses, categories, assignments, grades, and settings you enter are stored only in a local database on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not sent to Gospro LLC, to any third party, or to any server
  • is only accessible to you, on your device
  • is deleted if you uninstall the app

Reminders (notifications)

The app can remind you before an assignment is due. These are local notifications: your device schedules and shows them, using the assignment names, due dates and course names already stored on it. Nothing is sent to Gospro LLC or any server, and the app uses no push notification service.

  • The app asks for permission to show notifications the first time you use reminders. You can say no and keep using the app without reminders.
  • Reminders can be turned off in the app’s Settings, set per assignment, or blocked in your device’s notification settings at any time.
  • On Android the app can also restart your scheduled reminders after the device restarts (it uses the “receive boot completed” permission only for that).

Purchases (APlusPlan Pro)

APlusPlan Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). The store handles the payment and tells the app only whether Pro has been bought. Gospro LLC does not receive your payment details, name, email or any purchase history, and the app keeps just a “Pro is unlocked” flag on your device. The store’s own privacy policy applies to the purchase, and its purchase library may send technical diagnostics to the store operator.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, and has no technical ability to collect:

  • your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • your location
  • advertising identifiers
  • usage analytics or crash reports (the app contains no crash-reporting code; if you choose to share diagnostics with Google or Apple in your device settings, those reports go to them, not to Gospro LLC)
  • any data from other apps or services

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third-party component that goes online is the store’s own purchase library (Google Play Billing on Android, StoreKit on iOS), used only for the Pro purchase described above.

Children’s privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect data), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: support@gosprollc.com
