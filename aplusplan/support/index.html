APlusPlan Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@gosprollc.com and include your phone model, its Android or iOS version, and the app version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app). Please don’t send screenshots of anything you want to keep private.

Your data

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. APlusPlan has no accounts and no servers, so Gospro LLC cannot see or recover your courses and grades. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Not yet. Because nothing is stored online and the app is not included in Android’s cloud backup, a new phone starts empty. Export and backup are planned for a future version.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app, which removes everything stored on the device. You can also delete individual courses, terms and assignments inside the app.

Grades and GPA

How is my course grade worked out?

Each category’s grade is the points you earned divided by the points possible. The course grade is the average of the categories that have scores, weighted by each category’s weight. A category with no scores yet is left out until it has one, so the grade is always “out of the work graded so far”. Extra credit adds percentage points on top.

My professor drops the lowest score. Can the app do that?

Yes. Open the course, choose Edit category on the category, and set Drop lowest scores (1, 2 or 3). The lowest scores by percentage stop counting, and appear crossed out and marked Dropped. At least one score always counts.

My GPA or letter grade looks wrong.

Check three things: the category weights add up to 100%, each course has the right number of credits, and the grade scale in Settings matches your school’s (for example A+ as 4.0 or 4.3). You can edit the scale at any time.

How do I see what I need on the next assignment or the final?

Each category shows what the next score needs to be to reach your target, and the Upcoming list on the Courses tab shows it per assignment. The target grade solver (part of Pro) works it out across all the work you have left.

Reminders

I’m not getting reminders.

Check that reminders are on in Settings and that notifications are allowed for APlusPlan in your phone’s settings. On Android, battery-saver modes can delay or hold notifications, and reminders are delivered with Android’s flexible timing, so one can arrive a few minutes after the time you chose. After a restart the app sets your reminders again by itself.

Can I choose when I’m reminded?

Yes. Choose a default lead time and time of day in Settings, or set a different reminder on each assignment: 30 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day, or a custom time before the due date.

APlusPlan Pro

What is Pro?

A one-time purchase, with no subscription and no ads. It lets you save What-If scenarios, compare them side by side, and use the target grade solver. Everything else in the app is free, including tracking grades, GPA, targets, reminders, dropping the lowest scores, and editing the grade scale.

I bought Pro on another phone, or reinstalled the app.

Open Settings → APlusPlan Pro and tap Restore purchase, signed in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store, so refunds go through them: Google Play’s order history for Android, or reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone.
