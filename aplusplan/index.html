APlusPlan

Grade Calculator · track assignments, calculate grades, hit your GPA target.

What it does

  • Add courses and assignment categories with your own weights
  • Log assignment scores and watch your course grade update automatically
  • Set a target grade per course and see exactly what you need to reach it
  • Track GPA per term and overall, including past terms you enter manually
  • Extra credit support, for a single assignment or a whole course
  • Light and dark mode

Private by design

APlusPlan works entirely offline. There are no accounts, no ads, and no data collection of any kind. Everything you enter stays on your device. Read the privacy policy.
