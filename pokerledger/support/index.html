Poker Ledger Support

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@gosprollc.com and include your phone model and app version (Settings in the app). Please don’t send backup files; they contain your players’ names.

Common questions

Where is my data?

Only on your phone. There are no accounts and no servers, so Gospro LLC cannot see or recover your games.

How do I back up my data or move to a new phone?

In Settings, use Back up now, then Share the file to Files, a cloud drive, or yourself. On the new phone, install the app and use Choose a backup file to restore it. The app also keeps a daily automatic backup on the device, but uninstalling the app removes those too, so share a copy somewhere safe.

Does the app move money?

No. It only works out who owes whom. Payments happen outside the app (cash, Venmo, Zelle and so on) and you mark them as paid.

The final chip count doesn’t match.

The app compares the chips counted with the chips issued (buy-ins and re-entries). Check for a missed re-entry or a miscounted stack before settling.

How do I delete my data?

Uninstall the app, which removes everything stored on the device.
