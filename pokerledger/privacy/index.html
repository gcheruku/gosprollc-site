Privacy Policy for Poker Ledger

Last updated: October 2026

Poker Ledger ("the app") is developed by Gospro LLC.

Summary

Poker Ledger does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The app has no network access, no analytics, no advertising, and no account system.

Data the app stores

The player names, group names, game dates and titles, buy-ins, chip counts, payments you mark as paid, and settings you enter are stored only in a local database on your device. This data:

  • never leaves your device unless you choose to share it
  • is not sent to Gospro LLC, to any third party, or to any server
  • is only accessible to you, on your device
  • is deleted if you uninstall the app

Backups and sharing

The app can create backup files and spreadsheet exports of your data, and can share results as text or as a picture. Backups are saved on your device. When you share a file or result, you choose the destination (for example a messaging app, Files, or a cloud drive) each time. What happens to it afterwards is governed by the app or service you chose. Gospro LLC never receives it.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any permissions. It has no internet access, and it does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

Data the app does not collect

The app does not collect, and has no technical ability to collect:

  • your name, email address, or any personal identifiers
  • your location
  • advertising identifiers
  • usage analytics or crash reports
  • any data from other apps or services

We do not sell or share information with anyone, because we do not have any.

Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Money

The app records who owes whom, but it never processes payments, holds funds, or connects to any payment service. Settling up happens outside the app.

Children’s privacy

The app is a scorekeeping tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect data), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: support@gosprollc.com
